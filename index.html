Banned 21 Records is an independent label for bands with something to say, and apparently it needs to be said loudly. Different bands. Different sounds. Zero enthusiasm for acting your age.

04 // The people behind the noise

B21 // Founding team

THE PEOPLE
IN CHARGE.

Three co-founders. One independent record label.

01 / 03

Mónica Argote

Co-Founder & CEO

She was an engineer. She didn't like it. So she did what any reasonable person would do: quit everything and start a record label. Now she runs Banned 21 Records and tries to make our worst ideas sound fantastic.

02 / 03

Roberto Rodríguez

Co-Founder & Talent Manager

He started out managing small bands, the kind that played to twenty people and got paid in beer. One tiny venue after another, armed with contacts nobody quite knows how he got, he ended up at Banned 21 Records. Now he scouts talent and looks after the bands. Curiously, he's never around when the amps need carrying.

03 / 03

Sabine «Sabi» Satrustegi

Co-Founder & Head of PR

She used to be a journalist. Now she makes sure other journalists talk about our bands. She handles press, communications and promotion at Banned 21 Records. Making noise is easy. Getting someone to listen is another matter.
